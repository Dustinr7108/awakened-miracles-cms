      <h2>Program Modules (Professional Training)</h2>
      <p class="muted">
        These modules are designed to translate spiritual depth into professional competence, ethical practice, and responsible application.
      </p>

      <div class="hr"></div>

      <article class="section">
        <h3>Module 1: Psychic Professionalism – How to Give a Great Reading</h3>
        <p class="muted">
          This foundational module establishes professional standards for intuitive and spiritual counseling work. Students learn how to deliver clear,
          ethical, and grounded readings while maintaining appropriate boundaries and client safety. Emphasis is placed on discernment, responsibility,
          and avoiding projection or dependency.
        </p>
        <p class="small"><strong>Key outcomes:</strong> Conduct responsible readings; maintain ethical/energetic boundaries; communicate insights without causing harm.</p>
      </article>

      <article class="section">
        <h3>Module 2: Consciousness and YOU</h3>
        <p class="muted">
          Develop practitioner self-awareness by exploring states of consciousness and the role of the counselor as observer rather than authority.
          Learn how beliefs, emotional states, and unconscious material influence sessions—and how to remain grounded, present, and neutral while supporting others.
        </p>
        <p class="small"><strong>Key outcomes:</strong> Increased self-regulation; reduced projection; stronger presence and professional grounding.</p>
      </article>

      <article class="section">
        <h3>Module 3: Channeling (Discernment & Responsible Application)</h3>
        <p class="muted">
          Channeling is approached through discernment, ethics, and accountability. This module teaches how to evaluate intuitive information,
          communicate responsibly, and recognize when channeling is appropriate—or when it is not—so sessions remain grounded and safe.
        </p>
        <p class="small"><strong>Key outcomes:</strong> Ethical use of intuitive info; discernment between insight and imagination; safe communication practices.</p>
      </article>

      <article class="section">
        <h3>Module 4: Shamanism (Symbolic Healing Frameworks)</h3>
        <p class="muted">
          Explore shamanic principles as symbolic frameworks rather than role adoption. Learn how to responsibly apply concepts without cultural misuse,
          spiritual inflation, or mythologizing—keeping the focus on grounded counseling value.
        </p>
        <p class="small"><strong>Key outcomes:</strong> Ethical use of symbolic models; clear boundaries around spiritual roles; practical application.</p>
      </article>

      <article class="section">
        <h3>Module 5: Soul Encounters (Grief, Loss, and Spiritual Experience)</h3>
        <p class="muted">
          Learn how to support individuals reporting spiritual experiences around grief, loss, or perceived contact. Focus is placed on emotional containment,
          meaning-making, and psychological safety—without reinforcing interpretations that may destabilize clients.
        </p>
        <p class="small"><strong>Key outcomes:</strong> Support grief-related phenomena safely; hold space without reinforcing delusion; facilitate integration.</p>
      </article>

      <article class="section">
        <h3>Module 6: Counseling Stabilization & Safety Tools (Heads Up Tools)</h3>
        <p class="muted">
          Practical tools for grounding, stabilization, and emotional regulation during sessions. Learn how to respond when clients become overwhelmed,
          dysregulated, or dissociated—so your work remains contained, safe, and professionally responsible.
        </p>
        <p class="small"><strong>Key outcomes:</strong> De-escalation methods; session stabilization strategies; increased client safety and trust.</p>
      </article>

      <article class="section">
        <h3>Module 7: Dream Play (Therapeutic Dream Integration)</h3>
        <p class="muted">
          Work with dreams as symbolic material rather than predictive authority. Learn client-led exploration methods, how to avoid suggestibility,
          and how to support integration without imposing interpretation.
        </p>
        <p class="small"><strong>Key outcomes:</strong> Facilitate safe dream exploration; avoid projection/suggestion; use dreams as integration tools.</p>
      </article>

      <article class="section">
        <h3>Module 8: Meditation and Prayer (Grounded Spiritual Practices)</h3>
        <p class="muted">
          Learn non-dogmatic meditation and prayer practices that support nervous system regulation, clarity, and grounding. Students learn how to guide
          stabilizing practices and adapt them to individual needs—avoiding spiritual bypassing.
        </p>
        <p class="small"><strong>Key outcomes:</strong> Guide safe grounding practices; support regulation; avoid bypassing and destabilizing approaches.</p>
      </article>

      <article class="section">
        <h3>Module 9: Mental Telepathy (Intuitive Empathy & Boundaries)</h3>
        <p class="muted">
          “Telepathy” is reframed as intuitive empathy and heightened perception—handled with ethical boundaries. Learn how to avoid mind-reading assumptions,
          reduce misinterpretation, and communicate with precision without reinforcing dependency.
        </p>
        <p class="small"><strong>Key outcomes:</strong> Distinguish intuition from projection; maintain ethical communication boundaries; reduce overreach.</p>
      </article>

      <article class="section">
        <h3>Module 10: Time Travel (Symbolic Timeline & Trauma-Informed Work)</h3>
        <p class="muted">
          Time-based experiences are approached as symbolic narrative processes. Learn to work with memory and personal timelines responsibly—without
          false-memory creation—using grounding, consent, and trauma-informed framing.
        </p>
        <p class="small"><strong>Key outcomes:</strong> Support timeline exploration safely; avoid false-memory risk; facilitate trauma-informed integration.</p>
      </article>

      <article class="section">
        <h3>Module 11: Out of Body Journeys (Altered States & Integration)</h3>
        <p class="muted">
          Learn to support clients reporting altered-state experiences while prioritizing grounding and reintegration. This module emphasizes recognizing
          dissociation indicators, maintaining containment, and knowing when referral is appropriate.
        </p>
        <p class="small"><strong>Key outcomes:</strong> Identify dissociation vs spiritual experience; support reintegration; apply appropriate referral decisions.</p>
      </article>

      <article class="section">
        <h3>Module 12: Integration & Readiness to Practice</h3>
        <p class="muted">
          The capstone module focuses on integration, ethical readiness, and professional self-assessment. Students review case studies, clarify scope of practice,
          and develop a grounded framework for responsible real-world application.
        </p>
        <p class="small"><strong>Key outcomes:</strong> Assess readiness; clarify ethical scope/limits; receive Certificate of Completion.</p>
      </article>
